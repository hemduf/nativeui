# Contributing to NativeUI

NativeUI welcomes contributions that are compatible with the project's architecture, development workflow and dual-licensing model.

Technical contribution requirements are described in `AGENTS.md`. This document defines the legal terms that apply when you submit a contribution.

## 1. Why a contribution licence is required

NativeUI is offered under both the AGPL-3.0-only and commercial licences. To preserve that model, the project must have permission to distribute accepted contributions under both licensing paths.

A simple "inbound = outbound" rule is not sufficient for this project because a contribution licensed only under the AGPL could prevent the project from offering a complete commercial licence later.

This is a **licence grant, not a copyright assignment**. You retain copyright in your original contribution.

By submitting a contribution, you represent that:

- you have the legal right to make the contribution and grant the licences above;

Do not submit code copied from projects whose licence is incompatible with NativeUI's dual-licensing model unless the Licensor has explicitly approved the inclusion and the required notices are preserved.

Unless separately agreed in writing, contributors are not required to provide support, maintenance or future updates for their contributions.

## 6. How acceptance occurs

These terms apply when you intentionally submit a pull request, patch, commit, issue attachment or other code/documentation contribution for inclusion in NativeUI and the submission clearly references this repository or project.

By submitting such a contribution, you indicate agreement to these contribution terms. If you cannot grant the rights described here, do not submit the contribution; contact the project maintainer first to discuss whether a separate written arrangement is possible.

When adding or updating a dependency, bundled asset, generated file or other third-party material, update `THIRD_PARTY.md` and preserve all notices required by the applicable upstream licence.
