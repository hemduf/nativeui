# NativeUI Legal Index

NativeUI uses a JUCE-style dual-licensing structure: an open-source licence for users who can comply with its copyleft obligations, and a separate commercial licence for proprietary products.

## Licence paths

### Open source

Use NativeUI under the [GNU Affero General Public License version 3](LICENSE.md) (`AGPL-3.0-only`). Users choosing this path must comply with the AGPL, including its source-availability requirements and its requirements for modified software used over a network.

### Commercial

A proprietary or otherwise non-AGPL use requires both:

1. acceptance of the [NativeUI Commercial End User Licence Agreement](EULA.md); and
2. a valid Commercial Order or other written commercial licence grant from the Licensor.

The Commercial Order is where pricing, seats, term, maintenance/update rights and negotiated exceptions belong. The repository EULA intentionally does not hard-code product tiers or prices.

## Commercial-order minimum fields

A commercial order should identify at least:

- legal name of the Licensee;
- NativeUI version or version family covered;
- number of Framework User seats or alternate licence metric;
- perpetual or subscription term;
- price, currency, taxes and payment schedule;
- update/maintenance period, if any;
- support entitlement, if any; and
- any exception to the standard EULA, such as framework redistribution, SDK/code-generator use, group-company coverage or special sublicensing rights.

## Dependency boundary

The NativeUI commercial licence covers only rights controlled by the NativeUI Licensor. Pugl, Skia, skia-builder, CPM.cmake and their transitive components retain their own upstream licences and notices.
