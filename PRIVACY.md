# NativeUI Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 6 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be processed by the NativeUI project and its licensor in connection with commercial licensing, support and NativeUI-operated online services.

For the purposes of this policy, **Licensor** means Fabrizio Duhem, acting as the NativeUI licensor and project maintainer.

## 1. The NativeUI framework itself

NativeUI is a C++ UI framework. The Framework itself does **not intentionally transmit telemetry, analytics, licence-check data or personal data to the Licensor as a condition of normal use**.

Applications and products built with NativeUI may collect or process data according to their own design. Their developers and operators are responsible for providing any privacy disclosures and obtaining any permissions required by applicable law. Using NativeUI does not make the Licensor the data controller or processor for data independently collected by those products.

Third-party dependencies may have their own behavior and policies. See `THIRD_PARTY.md` and the relevant upstream documentation.

## 2. Data that may be collected outside the Framework

Depending on how you interact with the project, the Licensor may receive or process:

- identity and contact information that you voluntarily provide;
- company or organisation information supplied for commercial licensing;
- billing, invoice and transaction records associated with a commercial licence;
- licence scope, seat count, licence term and entitlement information;
- support requests, bug reports, feature requests and related correspondence;
- technical information you choose to provide for diagnosis, such as operating-system version, compiler version, logs, crash traces or reproduction projects;
- contribution metadata and public profile information associated with Git commits, issues or pull requests; and
- security-report information that you voluntarily submit.

The Licensor does not require you to provide unrelated personal data in order to use NativeUI under the AGPL.

## 3. GitHub and other third-party services

The NativeUI repository is hosted on GitHub. When you access or interact with GitHub, GitHub may independently collect and process data under its own terms and privacy policy. GitHub is a separate service provider and its data processing is not controlled by this repository policy.

The same principle applies to payment processors, email providers, file-hosting providers or other third-party services that may later be used for licensing or support. Where such a provider is used, it may process data under its own legal terms in addition to acting as a service provider to the Licensor.

## 4. Purposes of processing

Personal data may be used when necessary to:

- provide, administer and document commercial licences;
- process payments, invoices and accounting records;
- answer support, licensing, security and technical enquiries;
- maintain project history and attribution;
- investigate bugs, abuse, licence misuse or security incidents;
- operate and improve NativeUI-related services;
- comply with legal, tax, accounting or regulatory obligations; and
- establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

Personal data will not be sold as a standalone commercial product.

## 5. Legal bases under GDPR

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation or equivalent European data-protection law applies, processing may rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

- **contract** — processing necessary to enter into or perform a commercial licence or support agreement;
- **legal obligation** — processing required for tax, accounting, regulatory or lawful-disclosure obligations;
- **legitimate interests** — for project security, fraud prevention, support, service improvement, maintaining development history and protecting legal rights, where those interests are not overridden by your rights; or
- **consent** — where consent is specifically requested, for example for optional marketing communications.

## 6. Sharing and processors

Personal data may be shared only as reasonably necessary with service providers that support licensing, payments, hosting, communications, accounting, legal compliance or security, and with public authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

Public contributions, issue comments and pull-request discussions should be treated as public information when submitted to a public repository. Do not include secrets, private credentials, confidential customer data or unnecessary personal data in public project content.

## 7. International transfers

Some service providers, including source-code hosting providers, may process data outside your country or outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, reasonable legally recognised safeguards will be used, such as adequacy decisions or approved contractual mechanisms provided by the relevant service provider.

## 8. Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including applicable legal, tax, accounting, support, security and dispute-resolution periods.

Git repository history is intentionally durable. Public contribution metadata may remain in distributed repository history even after an account is deleted because preserving authorship and project provenance is a legitimate and technically necessary function of distributed version control. Where legally required and technically feasible, specific personal information may be corrected or removed without corrupting repository integrity.

## 9. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal data under the Licensor's control. No internet-based system can be guaranteed absolutely secure, and you should avoid sending credentials, secrets or unnecessary sensitive data in support requests or public project discussions.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have rights to:

- request access to personal data held about you;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
- request erasure in qualifying circumstances;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- receive portable data where the right applies;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
- lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

Identity verification may be required before a request is fulfilled. Some data may need to be retained where required by law or where another lawful basis applies.

## 11. Children

NativeUI is a software-development framework and its licensing and project services are not directed at children. The Licensor does not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children through NativeUI licensing or support services.

Developers who use NativeUI in products directed at children remain responsible for complying with COPPA, GDPR requirements relating to children and any other applicable law.

## 12. Marketing

Commercial or project marketing communications will be sent only where permitted by applicable law. Where consent is the legal basis, you may withdraw consent at any time. Transactional, licence, security or support communications may still be sent where necessary to provide a requested service or fulfil a legal obligation.

## 13. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be documented in the repository. Changes apply prospectively from their stated effective date and do not reduce rights granted by mandatory law.

## 14. Contact

For privacy or data-protection requests, contact the Licensor using a non-public contact method published by the NativeUI repository owner/project maintainer or the contact details contained in your Commercial Order. **Do not post privacy requests containing personal or sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.**
